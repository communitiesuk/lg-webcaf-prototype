CAF for Local Government

Use this service to help your council complete one-off onboarding and council setup, then carry out the annual CAF assessment.

CAF helps organisations assess and improve cyber resilience. For local government, it supports a structured view of organisational resilience and critical systems.

What this service helps you do

  • complete controlled council onboarding and setup
  • assign the initial CAF lead and authorised users
  • define and maintain CAF scope over time
  • complete this year’s self-assessment after onboarding is done
There is one webCAF account per council. If your council already uses webCAF, ask to join that account instead of creating another one.

Before you begin

You only need enough information to start council onboarding. Detailed guidance stays outside this page.

  • make sure you are using your approved council or civil service email address
  • know who should act as CAF lead and who needs access first
  • read the NCSC Cyber Assessment Framework overview if you need background
