We are checking your registration

This is a one-time check because you are the first person from {{ councilName }} to register. It is not a check on you as an individual.

You will not be able to see your council record until the check is done.

Colleagues who join later will not need to go through this check. Once your council is registered, they can ask to join. Your council’s CAF Lead can approve their access.

What happens next

MHCLG will check your council and email address. This usually takes up to [n] working days. We will email you at {{ email }} when the check is complete or if we need more information.

If you have not had an email from us after [n] working days, contact the CAF team at caf@localdigital.gov.uk.
