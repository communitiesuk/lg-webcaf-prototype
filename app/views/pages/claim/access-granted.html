What happens next

Your council’s CAF Lead manages the CAF for local government on behalf of your council. They will set up your council to use this digital service and decide who can access it.

If you are the CAF Lead, you can start straight away. If someone else is the CAF Lead, you can invite them at the next step. Nobody can see your council record until your CAF Lead has accepted your invitation.
