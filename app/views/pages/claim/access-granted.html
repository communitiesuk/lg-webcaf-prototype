What happens next

You can now:

  • work on the whole council record
  • see any earlier CAF assessment information MHCLG holds for your council
  • invite colleagues to use this service
  • approve requests from colleagues who ask to join, until your council names its CAF Lead

Being the first to register does not make you CAF Lead for your council. Naming the CAF Lead is the first task when you set up your council. Once a CAF Lead is named, they approve requests to join instead of you.
