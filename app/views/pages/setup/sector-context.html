Council mission

To enhance the wellbeing and prosperity of a diverse community by providing responsive and accountable government services, fostering economic growth, and promoting a safe and thriving environment for residents.

Council objectives

  1. Stimulate economic growth and job creation.
  2. Promote the health, safety, and quality of life of residents.
  3. Improve the council's infrastructure to support sustainable growth and development.

Threat actors that could attack the council

These include:

  • cyber-criminal organisations
  • nation-state actors
  • insider threats
  • hacktivist groups

Why the council is a target

  1. It handles a vast amount of sensitive information, making it an attractive target for cybercriminals seeking to steal valuable data or disrupt operations.
  2. It is reliant on external suppliers, shared systems and digital services to deliver council services.
  3. Disrupting council services could have significant repercussions on public safety, economic stability, and community wellbeing.

Potential consequences of successful attacks

These include:

  • data breaches
  • service disruption
  • harm to residents
  • loss of data availability or integrity
  • financial and organisational impact
