What this outcome is about

This outcome is about making sure people are who they say they are, use their own account, and only get the access they need. It also covers stronger controls for higher-risk access, such as privileged access and remote access.

What you may need

  • joiner, mover and leaver processes for this system
  • role profiles or access control information
  • how multi-factor authentication, remote access and privileged access are managed
  • recent access reviews, audit checks or control evidence

Who may need to contribute

  • service owner or system owner
  • identity and access management or infrastructure lead
  • supplier or managed service contact where access is externally administered
  • information security or audit colleagues who can confirm evidence
