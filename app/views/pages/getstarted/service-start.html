Assess and manage your council’s cyber resilience

Use this service to complete the Cyber Assessment Framework (CAF) for local government.

You can:

  • record your essential services and the systems they depend on
  • decide what you will self-assess this cycle
  • complete your self-assessment with colleagues
  • securely share your assessment and evidence with an independent assurer
  • create and manage your improvement and implementation plan

On the next page you can sign in with your council email address. If you need to use a non-council email address, contact the CAF team at caf@localdigital.gov.uk to request access.

Before you start

You will need:

  • your council email address, which you will use to sign in
  • roughly 26 weeks to complete a full cycle, including assurance
  • time for calls and workshops with an independent assurer
