Assess and manage your council’s cyber resilience

Use this service to complete the Cyber Assessment Framework (CAF) for your council, share it with an independent assurer, and plan the improvements that follow.

You can:

  • record your essential services and the systems they depend on
  • complete your self-assessment with colleagues
  • share evidence securely with your assurer
  • keep your improvement plan in one place

Before you start

You will need:

  • a work email address at your council
  • a GOV.UK One Login, or you can create one when you start
  • roughly 26 weeks to complete a full cycle, including assurance
  • time for calls and workshops with your assurer, which you book through Microsoft Bookings

Before you begin, you may want to read

  • How the CAF works and what is involved (security.gov.uk)
  • Making the case to your leadership team (security.gov.uk)
  • Who to involve at your council (security.gov.uk)
