What counts as a critical system

A critical system is a system, platform or technology service that an essential service depends on.

  • Use the system name people in the council would recognise.
  • Include supplier-hosted, shared-service and externally managed systems if your council depends on them.
  • Think about what would stop or seriously degrade service delivery if it became unavailable or was compromised.
  • Do not limit this list to systems owned directly by internal IT.

Example: a revenues platform managed by a supplier can still be one of your critical systems if residents and staff depend on it.
