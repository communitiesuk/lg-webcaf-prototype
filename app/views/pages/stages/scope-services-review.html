What counts as an essential service

An essential service is a service your council must keep running for residents, service users or staff.

  • Examples include waste collection, adult social care, benefits and housing support.
  • Use a service name people in your council will recognise.
  • Do not include teams, projects or systems here.
  • If you are naming software, a platform or a supplier service, add it later as a critical system.

Example: Adult social care is an essential service. A case management platform that supports it is a critical system.
