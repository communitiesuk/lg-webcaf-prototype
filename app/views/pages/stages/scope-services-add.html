What counts as an essential service

An essential service is something residents, service users or the council rely on the authority to keep delivering.

  • Examples include waste collection, adult social care, benefits administration and housing support.
  • Use the service name people in the council would recognise.
  • Do not add teams, projects, software or infrastructure here.
  • If you are naming a platform, supplier or technology, add it later as a critical system instead.

Example: "Adult social care" is an essential service. "Liquidlogic" or "case management platform" is a critical system that supports that service.
