Help with this step
  • Prioritise systems that would have the biggest impact on the council if disrupted by a cyber attack.
  • Use your team's expertise to decide this, including service leads, system owners, and IT or cyber colleagues where needed.
  • Be ready to explain the rationale for the priority you choose to your assurer.

Base the decision on service impact, dependency and recoverability, not just cost or profile.

  • High: major disruption to critical or statutory services, little workaround, high operational or resident impact.
  • Medium: important impact, but workarounds or slower recovery may be possible.
  • Low: more limited impact or easier recovery.
  • Not sure yet: use this only if you genuinely need more input from service or system owners.

Example: a platform supporting benefits payments may be high priority if disruption would quickly affect vulnerable residents and there is no practical fallback.
