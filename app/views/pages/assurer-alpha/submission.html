Submission route exploration

This page explores an alpha option for submitting the full CAF submission to MHCLG.

In the standard model, the council submits the CAF assessment after assurance. This prototype explores an alternative where the assurer submits on the council’s behalf, while keeping the assurer’s independent position separate from the council submission.
